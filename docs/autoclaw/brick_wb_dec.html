The Brick Club List v1.0

This is a volunteer-maintained database of Xteink firmware.
Our goal is to bring compatibility information together into one easy-to-understand website so owners can flash firmware that enhances their experience. We hope this will also avoid bricking devices due to compatibility issues.

We do our best to keep this information accurate and up to date, but we can't guarantee 100% accuracy.
Do your own research before flashing any firmware.

This site is not affiliated with or endorsed by Xteink. We do not endorse one firmware over another.
